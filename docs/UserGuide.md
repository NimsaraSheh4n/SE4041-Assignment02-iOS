# Smart Campus Navigator - User Guide

Smart Campus Navigator helps students, staff, visitors and new campus users find campus facilities, view them on a map and save the ones they use most.

---

## 1. Home Screen

The Home screen is the first screen you see. It shows:

- the app title and tagline
- a **Search locations** field
- **Quick Access** buttons for **Campus Map** and **Saved Places**
- the **Campus Locations** list

Tap any location card to open its details.

## 2. Searching

1. Tap the **Search locations** field.
2. Type part of a location name or category (for example "Library").
3. The list updates as you type. Capitalisation does not matter.
4. Tap the **x** button to clear the search.

If nothing matches, a "No Locations Found" message appears.

## 3. Campus Map

1. On Home, tap **Campus Map**.
2. Drag to pan and pinch to zoom. A compass and scale bar are shown.
3. Tap a marker to see a preview card with the place name, category and description.
4. Tap **View Place Details** on the card to open the full details, or tap the **x** on the card to close it.

### Category Filtering

A row of category buttons sits at the top of the map: All, Library, Lecture Hall, Laboratory, Cafeteria, Administration and Student Services. Tap one to show only that kind of location; tap **All** to show everything again.

## 4. Place Details

Opened from the Home list, the map preview card or Saved Places. It shows:

- the place name and category
- an **About** description
- a **Location** map preview
- the latitude and longitude

## 5. Saving and Removing Favourites

- On Place Details, tap **Save to Favourites** to save the place. The button changes to **Saved to Favourites**.
- Tap the button again to remove the place from your favourites.

Saved places are stored on the device with Core Data.

## 6. Saved Places

1. On Home, tap **Saved Places**.
2. Tap a saved place to open its details.
3. To remove one, swipe it left and tap **Delete**, or tap **Edit** and use the delete controls.

If you have not saved anything, an empty-state message explains how saved places will appear.

## 7. Accessibility and Basic Usage

- Controls have VoiceOver labels and hints.
- Icons and text use system text styles so they grow with your **Larger Text** setting (Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size).
- The app uses system colours, so it follows the device's light or dark appearance.
- Use the back button at the top left of each screen to return to the previous screen.

> Note: the locations and coordinates are representative sample data, and the app does not provide turn-by-turn directions.
